# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 25, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Cashly Lao.

Cashly Lao is a personal finance management application designed to help
users track income, expenses, accounts, budgets, and spending by category.

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal
information.

By using Cashly Lao, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect the following
information:

### Account Information

When you create an account (email/password, or "Continue with Google")
using Firebase Authentication, we collect:

- Email address
- Display name (if provided)
- Profile photo (when signing in with Google, if your Google account has one)
- A user ID generated by Firebase

### Financial Information

Information you enter into the application, including:

- Accounts you add (name, type, balance, and currency)
- Transactions (income, expenses, and transfers between your own accounts —
  amount, date, category, and any note you add)
- Categories you create or customize
- Budgets you set for a category and month

This information is stored securely in Firebase Cloud Firestore, under a
record tied to your own user ID — it is never visible to other users of
the app.

### Device and Crash Information

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash and error
reports (e.g. device model, OS version, app version, and the technical
details of what caused a crash) from the app as installed by real users.
This is automatically enabled in the version of the app you install from
the app store, and disabled during our own development and testing.

This information helps us diagnose and fix bugs — it does not include your
financial data.

### Usage Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used — for
example, which screens are visited and whether a feature (such as adding
an account, recording a transaction, setting a budget, or exporting a
report) was used at all. These events never include the financial details
you entered: no amounts, names, notes, or balances are sent to Firebase
Analytics, only the fact that the action happened and, where relevant, a
non-identifying category (e.g. that an expense — not its amount — was
recorded). Like Crashlytics, this is disabled during our own development
and testing and only active in the version of the app you install from
the app store.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide the application's core features
- Synchronize your financial data across your own devices
- Secure your account
- Diagnose and fix crashes and bugs
- Respond to customer support requests

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Data Storage

Your data is securely stored using Google Firebase services (Firebase
Authentication and Cloud Firestore).

Firebase may process data on servers located outside your country. Learn
more: <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information is only shared:

- with Google Firebase, for authentication and cloud storage
- with Google, when you choose to sign in with your Google account
- when required by law, or to protect legal rights or prevent fraud

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including:

- Firestore security rules that restrict every user's data to their own
  account, enforced server-side
- Encrypted communication (HTTPS) between the app and Firebase
- Firebase Authentication for account access

However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

## 7. Data Retention

Your information is retained while your account remains active.

If you delete your account (from within the app, under Profile → Delete
account), your accounts, transactions, categories, and budgets are
permanently deleted from our systems, along with your authentication
record. This action cannot be undone.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Cashly Lao is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not
knowingly collect information from children.

## 9. Your Rights

You may:

- Access and update your information directly within the app
- Delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly
  within the app (Profile → Delete account)
- Contact us regarding privacy concerns, or to request deletion of your
  data if you're unable to do so in-app

## 10. Third-Party Services

Cashly Lao uses the following third-party services:

- Google Firebase Authentication (including Google Sign-In)
- Google Cloud Firestore
- Firebase Crashlytics
- Firebase Analytics

Each service has its own privacy policy, governing how it processes data
on our behalf.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be
posted within the application or on our website.
